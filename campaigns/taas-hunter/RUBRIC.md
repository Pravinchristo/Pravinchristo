# TaaS sales hunter — screening rubric (v1, 2026-10-27)

Role: India-based new-business hunter for testing-as-a-service / managed QA, selling into US, APAC, Europe and/or domestic India.
SaaS is out. The hunter wins the full engagement in the first phase.

## Defaults (change any and re-score)
1. Full-scope threshold: first contract ≥ $150K TCV and ≥ 6 months (US/Europe); ≥ $75K (APAC); ≥ ₹50 lakh (domestic India).
2. Staff augmentation (T&M): counts only alongside fixed-price or managed-service experience. T&M-only = out.
3. Pilot that the same person converted to the full engagement within ~3 months = counts.
4. Hunter must have shaped scope and price (presales support allowed).

## Gates (fail any = out)
- G1 Services: most of their quota has been services contracts, not licences/subscriptions/usage.
- G2 Base: lives and works in India.
- G3 Hunting: evidence of a new-logo quota or new-logo wins (not only account growth).
- G4 Market: has sold into at least one of US, APAC, Europe, or domestic India.

## Score (100)
| Factor | Pts | Full marks | Partial |
|---|---|---|---|
| TaaS depth | 25 | Testing/QA services core | Adjacent managed services 15; crowdtesting / subscription QA 10 |
| Hunting evidence | 25 | Named new logos with numbers | Claims without numbers 15; hunt + farm mix 10 |
| Full-scope deals | 20 | Stated TCV above threshold; fixed-price, managed or SLA | RFP-led 15; not stated 8; pilots / T&M only 3 |
| Market coverage | 15 | 2+ international regions plus domestic | One international region 10–12; domestic only 8 |
| Scope & price ownership | 10 | Priced, scoped and negotiated SOW/MSA | Shared with presales 6; none shown 3 |
| Seniority fit | 5 | 8–20 years | Outside the range 2–3 |

Tiers: A ≥ 70, B 55–69, C 40–54, below 40 out.

## Gray-area rulings
- Managed testing using the provider's own tools: IN.
- Crowdtesting (Applause, Testlio, Test IO): GRAY, score at 10 for TaaS depth, verify contract type.
- QA sold as subscription (QA Wolf, Rainforest): GRAY leaning out.
- Tool reseller + implementation: IN only if services are the majority of deal value.
- Platform + services add-on (BrowserStack, Tricentis, LambdaTest, Testsigma, Qyrus): OUT.
- Empanelment / rate-card MSA "wins" without a scoped project: not a full-scope win.

## Screening question
"Walk me through your last three new logos: contract value and scope at signature, who priced it, who grew it afterwards, and what share of your quota came from new logos."
