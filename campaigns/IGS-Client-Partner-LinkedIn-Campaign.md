# LinkedIn Hiring Campaign: Client Partner, Enterprise Sales & Account Growth
**Company:** Intact Green Services (IGS) · **Location:** Bengaluru (Hybrid / Client-Facing) · **Experience:** 8–18 yrs

---

## 1. Campaign Strategy

**Goal:** 25–40 qualified applicants and 8–12 shortlist-ready hunters in 3 weeks.

**Who we want:** Hunters who have personally opened enterprise accounts in QE, Test Automation, DevOps, Digital Engineering or AI services, and can name the logo, the first executive contact, the TCV and the sales cycle.

**Who we don't want:** Account managers who mainly handled renewals, staffing or inherited accounts. Every post states this openly so people screen themselves out before applying.

**Core message:** *"Bring the logos you opened. Build the accounts you land."*

**Channels**
| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn Job Post (Promoted) | Inbound applications, with screening questions |
| Company Page posts (5-post series) | Brand + reach |
| Leadership / Recruiter personal posts | Higher reach than company page; warm network |
| Employee advocacy (sales, pre-sales, delivery leads) | Referrals |
| Recruiter InMail (sourcing) | Proactive outreach to passive hunters |
| Groups & comments | QE/Testing and IT services sales communities |

**Screening questions (enable "must-have"):**
1. How many years of enterprise IT services / tech consulting sales experience do you have? (Numeric, minimum 8)
2. Have you personally originated and closed a new enterprise logo in the last 3 years? (Yes/No, must be Yes)
3. Have you sold Quality Engineering, Test Automation, DevOps/DevSecOps, Digital Engineering, TDM or AI-led services? (Yes/No, must be Yes)
4. Have you built and presented pitch decks with pre-sales teams? (Yes/No, must be Yes)
5. Are you based in or willing to work from Bengaluru (hybrid)? (Yes/No)

**Promotion settings:** Budget about ₹1,500–2,500/day for 14–21 days. Targeting is set automatically from title and skills. Watch the applicant-quality ratio after day 5 and pause if fewer than 20% are qualified.

| Origination story | Clear outbound/referral/partner path + first exec contact |

| Service-line fit | QE / Automation / DevOps / DE / AI / Managed Services |

| Metric | Target |

## 10. Engager Follow-Up Playbook (comments, reactions, DMs)

**Check engagement 3 times a day for the first 72 hours** (about 9 AM, 1 PM and 6 PM IST). Reply to every comment within 2 hours. Fast replies keep the post in more feeds.

**Sort every engager into one of four groups first:**
| Group | How to spot them | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A: Strong fit | IT services or QE sales, 8–18 yrs, title like Client Partner / Account Director / Sales Director | Reply to comment + connect + DM within 24 hrs |
| B: Possible fit | Sales but product/SaaS, or under 8 yrs, or a farmer-only profile | Reply + connect, then ask the 5 screening questions |
| C: Referrer | Tagged someone, recruiter, or not in sales | Thank them + DM the person they tagged |
| D: Not relevant | Freshers, unrelated roles | Polite reply only |

### Comment replies (post publicly, under their comment)
- **"Interested" from a strong fit:** `Thanks {FirstName}! Sending you a connection request and a DM now with the details.`
